Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our freight services, customs, delivery times, and shipping prices.

General Questions

What freight services do you offer?

We offer comprehensive freight forwarding services including:

  • Sea Freight - FCL and LCL container shipping worldwide
  • Air Freight - Express cargo services globally
  • Land Transport - Road freight across Ghana and West Africa
  • Customs Clearance - Expert documentation and compliance

How long has Perfect Page Freight been in business?

Perfect Page Freight Logistics has been providing reliable freight forwarding services since 2015, giving us over 15 years of combined industry experience.

Shipping & Delivery

What are your typical delivery times?

Delivery times vary by service type:

  • Air Freight: 2-5 business days (depending on destination)
  • Sea Freight: 2-6 weeks (depending on route)
  • Land Transport: 1-7 days (depending on distance)

Contact us for specific transit times to your destination.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping costs depend on several factors including:

  • Type of cargo and weight
  • Destination and shipping route
  • Service type (air, sea, or land)
  • Additional services needed (customs, insurance, etc.)

Request a free quote for an accurate estimate.

Customs & Documentation

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes! We provide full customs clearance services. Our team of experts handles all documentation, duty calculations, and compliance requirements to ensure your cargo clears customs smoothly and efficiently.

What documents do I need for shipping?

Required documents typically include:

  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading (sea) or Air Waybill (air)
  • Certificate of Origin (if applicable)
  • Import/Export licenses (if applicable)

Our team will guide you through all documentation requirements.

Business Information

Is Perfect Page Freight Logistics a registered company?

Yes, Perfect Page Freight Logistics is a fully registered and licensed freight forwarding company operating in Ghana. We comply with all local and international shipping regulations.
