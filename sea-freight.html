Our Sea Freight Services

We offer comprehensive ocean freight services designed to meet your specific shipping needs. Whether you're shipping a full container or less than a container load, we have the right solution for you.

  • FCL (Full Container Load) — Exclusive use of a container for your cargo, ideal for large shipments
  • LCL (Less than Container Load) — Cost-effective sharing of container space for smaller shipments
  • Door-to-Door Delivery — Complete logistics from pickup at origin to delivery at final destination
  • Customs Clearance — Expert handling of all documentation and regulatory requirements
  • Container Tracking — Real-time visibility of your cargo throughout the journey
